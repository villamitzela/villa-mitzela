A six-bedroom family home overlooking the Pagasetic Gulf, with space for 14–18 guests. Just above the small seaside village of Mitzela (Amaliapolis), it is surrounded by gardens and has direct access to the sea. It has been a place for summers, gatherings, and celebrations for generations.
