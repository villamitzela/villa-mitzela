Writer’s Room ..............................................................................................

Located in the main house, the Writer’s Room is both a private retreat and a space for inspiration. The adjoining office, enclosed in glass, offers a panoramic 180° view of the garden and sea, making it the perfect place to work, read, or simply take in the surroundings. A single bed tucked into the office provides an extra place to rest without leaving the view behind.

The linked bedroom features a king-size bed, ample closet space, and a TV for added comfort. Its open-plan bathroom includes a bathtub, a walk-in shower, and a dedicated vanity area. With seamless indoor-outdoor flow and thoughtful design, this suite balances function and tranquility.

  • One king size bed

  • Dedicated workspace with views
  • Fireplace
  • Ensuite bathroom & vanity area

Suite ..............................................................................................

The panoramic room of the villa, the Suite offers an uninterrupted view of the village and sea from its private balcony. Inside, a fireplace adds warmth and character, making it a space for all seasons. The room is furnished with a king-size bed and an additional single bed, balancing comfort with versatility.

The ensuite bathroom is a highlight of its own, featuring a bathtub positioned beside a large window that frames the sweeping view. Thoughtfully designed, the Suite is an inviting blend of elegance and tranquility, perfect for both quiet mornings and slow evenings by the fire.
