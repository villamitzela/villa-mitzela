Villa Mitzela is available for weddings, baptisms, celebrations, and other special events, as well as film and photo shoots. Over the years, our own family has celebrated weddings and baptisms here, so the house has naturally become a place for bringing people together. With generous outdoor spaces, open views of the sea, and different settings throughout the property, it can easily adapt to anything from an intimate gathering to a larger celebration or production.
